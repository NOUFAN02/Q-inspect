System Architecture & Defense Layers

01

Optical CV Decoding

Computer Vision QRCodeDetector algorithm extracts raw bitstream matrices from damaged, scaled, or tilted QR codes.

02

SSRF Isolation Firewall

Blocks attacks targeting internal networks (RFC 1918), link-local IP addresses, and cloud provider metadata endpoints.

03

Redirect & Shortener Tracing

Unmasks URL shorteners and follows multi-hop redirect chains to reveal the true final destination before any visit.

04

Sandboxed Content Detonation

Renders the final page inside an isolated headless browser to detect brand impersonation, fake login forms, and cross-domain credential theft — regardless of hosting or domain age.

05

Multi-Factor Risk Scoring

Fuses infrastructure, domain freshness, TLS validity, structural heuristics, and live content signals into a single 0–100 verdict.
